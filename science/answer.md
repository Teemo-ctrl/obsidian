**Question:** In pea plants, round seeds (R) are dominant over wrinkled seeds (r), and yellow seeds (Y) are dominant over green seeds (y). Based on this information, determine whether each statement is True or False.

  

**Answers:**

  

1. Each parent can produce four types of gametes: RY, Ry, rY, and ry. → **Benar**
    
      
    
      
    
2. The expected phenotypic ratio is 9 round yellow : 3 round green : 3 wrinkled yellow : 1 wrinkled green. → **Benar**
    
      
    
      
    
3. The expected genotypic ratio contains 16 equally likely genotype combinations in the Punnett square. → **Benar**
    
      
    
      
    
4. All offspring are expected to have round yellow seeds because both parents show the dominant phenotypes. → **Salah**
    
      
    
      
    
5. The probability of producing an offspring with wrinkled green seeds (rryy) is 1/16. → **Benar**
